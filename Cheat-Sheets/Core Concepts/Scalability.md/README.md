# Scalability

## Definition

Scalability is the ability of a system to continue performing efficiently as demand increases.

---

## My First Thought

If the Internet keeps growing, one computer can't keep answering everyone's questions.

---

## Refined Understanding

A scalable system can grow without needing to be completely redesigned.

---

## My Analogy

Imagine owning a restaurant.

If 10 customers show up, everything works.

If 1,000 customers arrive, the restaurant shouldn't collapse.

A scalable restaurant grows by adding more cooks, tables, or even new locations instead of rebuilding the entire business.

---

## Real World Example

DNS.

Instead of one computer answering every request on Earth, many servers share the workload.
