# Project Engineering Zero X

Welcome to **Project Engineering Zero X**.

This repository documents my journey from beginner to cybersecurity and systems engineering through structured learning, hands-on labs, engineering principles, and real-world projects.

## Mission

Transform from someone who can follow instructions into an engineer who understands how computer systems work from the ground up.

## Objectives

- Build a deep understanding of computer systems.
- Connect theory with hands-on practice.
- Develop engineering thinking instead of memorization.
- Create real-world projects that demonstrate practical skills.
- Document continuous growth throughout the journey.

## Repository Structure

- **MIT** – Engineering theory and computer science
- **TryHackMe** – Hands-on labs and room notes
- **Python** – Programming and automation
- **Projects** – Portfolio and engineering builds
- **Cheat-Sheets** – Quick reference guides
- **Engineering-Principles** – Core concepts and ways of thinking
- **Journal** – Daily engineering log and reflections

## Philosophy

Every concept should answer three questions:

1. **What is it?**
2. **Why does it exist?**
3. **How does it work?**

If I can't answer all three, I keep learning.

## Motto

> **Learn it. Build it. Understand it. Teach it.**
