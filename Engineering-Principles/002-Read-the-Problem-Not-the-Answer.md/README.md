# Engineering Principle #002 - Read the Problem, Not the Answer

## The Principle

Never let the possible answers influence your understanding of the problem.

Understand the problem first.

---

## What I Learned

While completing a TryHackMe room, I immediately thought the correct answer was "containers."

I began doubting myself because the answer blanks appeared to match the length of "hypervisor."

The wording of the question mattered more than the number of blanks.

---

## Engineering Lesson

Read the problem.

Understand the problem.

Determine the answer.

Only then check whether it fits.

---

## Example

Question:

A company wants to host multiple small applications.

Correct thinking:

Small applications → Containers

Incorrect thinking:

The answer has ten letters, so it must be Hypervisor.

---

## Engineering Takeaway

Interfaces can mislead you.

Engineering starts by understanding the problem, not by guessing the answer.
